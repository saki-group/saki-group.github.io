Joining the Lab

Prospective Students

We are looking for motivated graduate and undergraduate students interested in research problems regarding spatial AI, knowledge integration, and data science.

Prospective graduate students should apply to the Institute of Computer Science and Engineering (資訊科學與工程研究所甲組), Institute of Data Science and Engineering (數據科學與工程研究所), Institute of Multimedia Engineering (多媒體工程研究所), Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Medical Computing (人工智慧與醫學計算研究所), or the Graduate Degree Program of Artificial Intelligence (人工智慧技術與應用碩士學位學程). Students who have been admitted and are interested in our lab are welcome to contact us.

NYCU undergraduate students from any department with relevant research interests are also welcome to contact us.

To help us better understand your background and research fit, please include the following information in your email. Brief descriptions are sufficient:

  1. Your CV/resume, or a summary of your academic background and relevant experience if you do not have one prepared.
  2. Your research interests and possible research directions you would like to explore.
  3. What you would most like to learn or develop in our lab.
  4. Your career goals or current plans after graduation.
  5. Why you think our lab would be a good fit for your interests and goals.
